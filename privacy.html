42,2

Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Version française (the French version prevails).

42.2 is a running training app built for running with family and friends. This page explains what data it uses, why, where it is stored and how you stay in control.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Elias De Bernardo, publisher of the 42.2 app. Contact: contact@422run.app.

No advertising data, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no data is ever sold or rented. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

3. Legal basis

  • Health data: your explicit consent (GDPR art. 9(2)(a)), given in the app before Apple Health is read. Withdraw it any time in Settings › Account and data › Withdraw health consent: your health data is then erased from our servers.
  • Account, plan, social features: performance of the service you request (art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Security and crash fixing: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Who sees what

  • Your family sees your sessions, load and sleep only if you turn on "Share with my family" (off by default).
  • Your groups see your first name, photo and running kilometres, never session details.
  • The global leaderboard shows your first name only if you join it.
  • During a guided session, your people see that you are running and your kilometres, so they can cheer you on.

5. Storage and providers

Data is stored with Supabase on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Providers involved: Supabase (database, sign-in, files; EU), Apple (Health on your device, Sign in with Apple, notifications), Google (Google sign-in if chosen; US), Expo / 650 Industries (push notifications and app updates; US), Resend (sign-in emails; US), OpenRouteService / HeiGIT (route computation, start and end points without your identity; Germany), OpenFreeMap and Esri (map and satellite tiles), Sentry (crash reports without health data; EU). Transfers outside the EU rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

42.2 does not send your data to any third-party AI. If an AI coach is added later, the app will ask for your separate consent first.

6. Retention

As long as your account exists. Deleting your account erases everything from the database immediately (technical backups expire within 30 days). Withdrawing health consent erases health data immediately; your account stays.

7. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, restriction and withdrawal of consent. Delete your account in the app (Settings › Account and data › Delete my account). For anything else, email contact@422run.app; we reply within one month. You may also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local authority.

8. Security, children, changes

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS); server-side access rules ensure each person only reaches their own data and what their people chose to share. 42.2 is for people aged 15 and over. If this policy changes significantly, the app will tell you before it applies.
